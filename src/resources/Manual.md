# WELLEYZ / MANUAL

## RANDOM / PRESETS
Select a needed SOUND TYPE, then press RANDOMIZE. The selected category sets the ranges for oscillators, envelope and tone; each run generates a new sound. SETTINGS controls whether Porta, FX, Arp, Voices and Background noise also change. Disabled groups retain their current values.

BACK restores the previous sound (up to 10 steps per instance). RESET restores the default parameters. SAVE writes a .wlyz preset; LOAD opens one from disk. User presets are stored in Documents/welleyz presets.

Select a preset in the PRESETS list to load it. Search filters by name. All, Likes and the category buttons filter the list; the heart marks a preset as liked. Preset categories are based on filenames.

## OSC
OSC 1–3 each have a power switch. WAVE selects the waveform; VOICES selects 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 or 8 unison voices. OCT, SEMI and FINE set pitch; LEVEL, PAN and DETUNE set loudness, stereo position and unison spread.

ENVELOPE controls attack, decay, sustain and release. FILTER sets cutoff and resonance. TUBE + LO-FI sets saturation (LAMP), bit depth (BITS), sample-rate reduction (CRUSH), vibrato rate (VIB-R) and depth (VIB-D). Each section has its own power switch. Background noise has a separate power switch, texture selector and LEVEL.

MASTER + ARP sets output VOLUME, arpeggiator mode (Off, Up, Down, UpDown) and rate (ARP-R). PORTA sets glide time. PORTA MODE Off disables glide; Always glides between notes; Legato glides when notes overlap. Double-click a knob to reset its value.

## FX
Switch each module on separately. FILTER selects lowpass, bandpass or highpass and sets cutoff, resonance and wet mix. HAZE adds stereo modulation; RATE, DEPTH and MIX control it. TAPS adds ping-pong delay; TIME, FEEDBACK and MIX set its repeats. SPACE adds reverb; SIZE, DAMP and MIX set its character.

In VST3, TAPS TIME is measured in seconds. In Standalone, it selects beat divisions based on the BPM setting.

## PLAY / DISPLAY
Play notes with the on-screen keyboard or MIDI. With the editor focused, computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; play from C5. PITCH bends notes and returns to center when released; MOD increases vibrato depth. Incoming MIDI Pitch Bend and CC1 also work. The knob beside the keyboard controls master volume.

COLORS selects the interface accent. The upper-right zoom control selects 50–200% and includes Refresh presets and Check for updates.

## STANDALONE
Select audio and MIDI devices in the Standalone application's settings. Open the metronome icon to set BPM (40–240) and turn the accented 4/4 click on or off. BPM also sets the timing of TAPS.

Press REC to record the synthesizer output without the click. Press STOP, then save a stereo WAV or discard the take. The save dialog starts in Documents/welleyz recordings.
